About

Aathi Elanchezhiyan is an aspiring visual storyteller, cinematographer, and filmmaker drawn to the quiet, unscripted moments most people scroll past — light breaking through monsoon clouds, a stray cat pausing at a puddle, a dead tree holding its shape against the sky.

Working across short films, observational footage, and still photography, Aathi treats the camera as a way of paying attention — building a visual language out of patience, contrast, and the textures of everyday India.
